# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and keep the space free of harassment and discrimination. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules and may ban repeat offenders.

**Reporting:** open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/mariovasco24/agentling/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct report" or contact the maintainer through the email on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/mariovasco24). Reports are handled privately.
